Featured Case Study · AI-Assisted Development

DMZ Loot Companion

An inventory and crafting planner developed from a real gameplay need, beginning as a multi-sheet Excel workbook and evolving into a web application prototype.

The problem: Tracking collected materials, recipe requirements, and multiple planned upgrades during gameplay was difficult and time-consuming.

My role: I defined requirements, tested the workbook during gameplay, reported defects, checked source data against recipes, and directed iterative changes with AI assistance. I evaluate generated results rather than assuming they are correct.

What I tested: Inventory quantities, ingredient name matching, calculated shortages, target-build selections, missing recipes, alphabetical sorting, and conditional highlighting. The project exposed formula and data-mapping issues that were corrected through repeated testing.

Current status: The spreadsheet is in active use. A browser-based prototype has been created, with additional features and hosting still in development. Public access and multi-user synchronization are not yet available.
