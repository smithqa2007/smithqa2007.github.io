Featured Event Production Case Study

Plano Virtual Comedy Festival 2020

How a three-person production team transformed an in-person comedy festival into a four-day virtual event during COVID-19.

100+ Performers
21 States
4 Countries
17 Shows
4 Days

When COVID-19 made an in-person festival impossible, I helped a three-person core production team rebuild the Plano Comedy Festival as a fully virtual event.

My responsibilities included performer selection, event scheduling, website QA, social media, promotional graphics, performer badges, volunteer coordination, attendee workflows, and live technical production. We used Zoom, OBS, Eventbrite, Discord, Powtoon, and custom digital assets to create a complete remote festival experience rather than simply placing comedians in video calls.
